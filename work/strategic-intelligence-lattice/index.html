RESULTS

Results

  • In the reported 96-context synthetic comparison, certificate portfolio matched the analytic feasible oracle in 47 of 96 contexts versus 31 of 96 for uniform at 576 generated cell queries, but averaged about 3.4 times the CPU; on the separately calibrated 48-context CPU holdout it matched 4 of 48 versus 12 of 48 for uniform.
