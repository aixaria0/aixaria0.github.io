02 / ACTUAL BASELINE

What the engine really does.

Human parameters enter a reproducible synthetic state machine. Candidate actions share seeded shocks, three hypothetical regimes are weighted, rollouts are evaluated, safety gates are applied, and only then is a synthetic action recorded.
